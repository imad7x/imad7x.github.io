Resume Studio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Resume Studio is an app for writing resumes and saving them as PDF files. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Your resumes stay on your phone

Everything you type into a resume – your name, contact details, work history, photo and so on – is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We do not run servers that receive your resumes, and the app has no accounts or sign-in. If your phone's own backup (Google backup) is turned on, Android may include the app's data in that backup, under your Google account and Android's backup rules.

When you import a file (for example a PDF or Word document), it is read on your device. When you import a photo or a scanned page, the text is recognised on your device with Google ML Kit through Google Play services; the picture is not uploaded by the app.

PDFs you create are saved where you choose, or shared with the app you pick. Deleting the app deletes its stored resumes.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, app interactions with ads, and diagnostic data. Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).

The content of your resumes is never used for advertising.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and is meant for people writing job applications.

Security

Your resumes are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Files you export are only as private as the place you save or share them to.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
